# Guidelines
## A. Cheating
All runs in Ranked and Casual modes must follow the [rules of the speedrun.com Minecraft Any% Glitchless leaderboard](https://rules.minecraftspeedrunning.com/latest).
1. Do not abuse Minecraft bugs disallowed under Section D.1.2 of the [speedrun.com rules](https://rules.minecraftspeedrunning.com/latest).
    - This will result in a queue ban in Ranked and Casual modes, ranging from 7 days to permanent.
2. Do not abuse bugs within the MCSR Ranked mod.
    - This will result in a ban from accessing MCSR Ranked, ranging from 30 days to permanent.
3. Do not attempt to inject cheats into MCSR Ranked.
    - This will result in a permanent ban from accessing MCSR Ranked.

## B. Matchmaking Abuse
MCSR Ranked works on Elo to provide balanced matchmaking to its players. The players are expected to uphold this, and not manipulate their Elo to be something that it isn't.

1. Do not lower your Elo outside of your skill range to play against easier opponents. (**Smurfing**)
    - Smurfing is defined as consistently playing at an Elo range where you significantly outpace your opponents in almost every game, negatively impacting the ranked experience for said opponents.
    - Continuing to lower your Elo will result in a queue timeout.
    - More intentional cases of manipulating Elo will result in a queue ban in Ranked and Casual modes, ranging from 3 days to 14 days.
2. Do not play Ranked or Casual modes on another player's account. (**Account sharing**)
    - For all involved, this will result in a queue ban in Ranked and Casual modes, ranging from 30 days to permanent.
3. Do not play Ranked or Casual modes on an alternative account, unless granted explicit permission from the admins. (**Alting**)
    - Alting is defined as repeatedly switching between several accounts and queuing Ranked or Casual modes.
    - Permanently switching from one account to another (e.g. if you lost access) will not be punished.
    - For the "main" account, this will result in a queue ban in Ranked and Casual modes, ranging from 30 days to permanent.
    - For any alternative accounts, this will result in a permanent queue ban in Ranked and Casual modes.
4. Do not repeatedly go AFK during your matches. (**AFKing**)
    - AFKing is defined as queuing a match with the intention of not playing the game.
    - This will result in a queue ban in Ranked and Casual modes, ranging from 1 day to 7 days.

## C. Player Conduct
In MCSR Ranked, the standard is to respect your opponent and ensure they have a good experience. As long as you maintain good sportsmanship, you don't need to worry.

1. Do not be excessively toxic, use slurs, discriminate, or post NSFW content in the game chat.
    - This will result in a chat mute, ranging from 7 days to permanent.
    - Out-of-game incidents will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.
    - Extremely serious incidents will result in the player being hidden in-game and from leaderboards.
2. Do not attempt to gain an unfair, informational advantage during your match, from your opponent's stream. (**Stream sniping**)
    - This will result in a queue ban in Ranked and Casual modes, ranging from 7 days to 30 days.

## D. Playoffs
Violations of the guidelines may result in a ban from attaining phase points or attending playoffs for a number of seasons, for more severe cases.
Violations during playoffs-related matches will be treated much more harshly.

A separate set of guidelines for playoffs-specific conduct is in the works. It will be linked here once it is published.

## E. Appeals and Reports
For now, appeals / information about your punishment should be via raising a ticket in the [MCSR Ranked Discord](https://mcsrranked.com/discord).
Successful appeals will reduce the duration of the punishment or lift the punishment entirely.

**Appealing a punishment does not mean it will be revoked. It means we will look again at your case, the context, and the way you conduct yourself.**

To report a player, you can click the "Report Players" button in the Ranked main menu (if you've matched against them).
Otherwise, you can raise a ticket in the [Discord](https://mcsrranked.com/discord).
