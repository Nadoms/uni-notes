**AI ethics** - What we decide is ethical regarding AI and robotics.
**Machine ethics** - How the robot can make its own ethical decisions.

## AI Ethics
**Deontological ethics** - Any true, moral law is universally applicable. In this world, robots would always make the objectively correct decision.
**Utilitarianism** - The greatest possible good for the greatest number of people. Maximise the average utility.
**Virtue ethics** - There is no right or wrong rule. Individuals are allowed to make decisions based on their morals. The dominant approach.

## Machine Ethics
The 4 levels of ethical complexity:
- Ethical impact agents - Machines which can be evaluated
- Implicit ethical agents - Machines which are designed to avoid unethical outcomes
- Explicit ethical agents - Machines which can reason about ethics
- Full ethical agents - Machines which can make moral judgments and justify them

## Responsible AI
**Responsible research and innovation (RRI)** - Involve society and its value in research early.
**eXplainable AI (XAI)** - Make AI non-blackbox without compromising performance.
